FAQ

Common Questions

Everything parents ask before enrolling.

We accept children from 1.5 years to 6 years of age. Pre-Nursery starts at 2 years, Nursery at 3, LKG at 4, and UKG at 5 years.

Our school operates from Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM. We follow a standard academic calendar with public holidays off.

Currently, we do not provide transport services. Parents are requested to arrange their own drop-off and pick-up.

We follow a play-based learning approach with a blend of Montessori and activity-based curriculum. Focus areas include literacy, numeracy, arts, music, and social skills.

Yes! Our entire campus is under 24/7 CCTV monitoring. The safety and security of your children is our top priority.

Our fees range from ₹20,000 to ₹40,000 per year depending on the class. We offer sibling discounts. Contact us for the exact breakdown.
